## Lesson Structure Template

Each lesson follows this structure:

1. **Learning Objectives** (3-5 clear goals)
2. **Core Concepts** (Theory and principles)
3. **Practical Examples** (Real-world scenarios)
4. **Exercises** (Actionable practice tasks)
5. **Common Mistakes** (What to avoid)
6. **Key Takeaways** (Summary points)
7. **Quiz** (5-10 questions to test understanding)
8. **Field Assignment** (Real-world practice task)

---

## Exercise Types

- **Mindset Exercises**: Journaling prompts, visualization, affirmations
- **Observation Exercises**: People-watching, IOI/IOD spotting
- **Practice Exercises**: Conversation starters, storytelling practice
- **Field Exercises**: Real-world approaches and interactions
- **Reflection Exercises**: Self-assessment, progress tracking

---

## Progression System

- **Lesson Completion**: Mark lessons as complete
- **Quiz Passing**: 70%+ to unlock next lesson
- **Field Reports**: Document real-world practice
- **Badges**: Earn achievements for milestones
- **Points**: Gain XP for engagement and practice
- **Streaks**: Maintain daily learning habits

---

## Content Guidelines

- **Practical over theoretical**: Every concept must have actionable steps
- **Ethical foundation**: Respect, consent, and authenticity emphasized throughout
- **Progressive difficulty**: Each lesson builds on previous knowledge
- **Real examples**: Use scenarios from training data and real experiences
- **Inclusive language**: Avoid objectification, promote genuine connection
- **Safety first**: Always emphasize reading signals and respecting boundaries
